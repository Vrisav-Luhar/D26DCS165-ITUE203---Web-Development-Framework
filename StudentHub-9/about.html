About StudentHub

StudentHub is a college student portal designed to provide a single interface for students, faculty and administrators.

Purpose

The portal organizes common academic and student-service activities in one place.

User Roles

Admin

Can access and manage student information and administrative functions.

Faculty

Can access permitted student information and academic functions such as attendance.

Student

Can view personal academic information and submit student-service requests.
