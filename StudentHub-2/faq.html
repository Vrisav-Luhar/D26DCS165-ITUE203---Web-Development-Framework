Frequently Asked Questions

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a college portal for students, faculty and administrators.

Who can use StudentHub?

Authorized students, faculty members and administrators can use the portal.

Can students edit their attendance?

No. Official attendance records are view-only for students.

Can students view their results?

Yes. Students can view their own academic results.

Can students submit grievances?

Yes. Students can submit and track grievances.
