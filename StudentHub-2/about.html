About StudentHub

A centralized digital platform for college students, faculty and administration.

Our Portal

StudentHub is designed to provide a single platform for managing and accessing important college-related information and services.


Students can access their academic information, attendance, results, leave requests, grievances, notifications and other services through their personal dashboard.


Faculty members can access authorized student information and perform academic tasks according to their permissions.

Portal Goals

  • Centralized student information
  • Easy academic access
  • Role-based access
  • Better communication
  • Responsive user interface

User Roles

Different users have different levels of access.

Admin

Manages student, faculty, event and system information and can access all student records.

Faculty

Accesses permitted student information and can manage authorized academic information.

Student

Accesses personal profile, attendance, results, leave requests, grievances and notifications.
